Aroma

On the nose, a harmonious interplay of the Aleksandrouli and Mujuretuli varieties, accompanied by intense aromas of ripe berries and juicy fruit, especially wild strawberry, blueberry and raspberry.

Taste

Light to medium-bodied, lively and fresh, with fine, piquant tannins and a harmonious balance of acidity and sweetness.

Serving & Pairing

Serve at 11–13°C. Ideal with Racha ham, light steaks, cheese and desserts.
