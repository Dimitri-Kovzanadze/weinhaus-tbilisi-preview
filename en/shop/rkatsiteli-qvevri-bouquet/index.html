Aroma

The varietal character of Rkatsiteli is intensely expressed, accompanied by a complex bouquet of dried herbs and flowers, rounded out by fine notes of wood and spice.

Taste

Velvety and elegant on the palate, with ripe, soft tannins, above-average acidity and balanced alcohol; a long, lingering finish.

Serving & Pairing

Serve at 13–16°C. Ideal with hearty Georgian cheese and meat dishes.
